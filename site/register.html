Liability Waiver & Release

Please read this carefully. By checking the waiver box on the registration form, you are giving up certain legal rights, including the right to sue for injuries described below.

1. Season fee and refunds

The season fee is $100, and it includes your league jersey. If you withdraw before your jersey has been purchased, you can get a full refund. Once your jersey has been purchased, the fee is completely non-refundable, for any reason, including injury, missed games or leaving the league. Fee assistance is available; ask the league office.

2. Assumption of risk

Basketball is a physical, competitive sport. I understand that taking part in Smithtown Gospel Tabernacle Basketball League games, practices, drafts and events, and being on the church’s premises, carries a risk of injury. Those risks include collisions with other players, falls, slips, being struck by the ball, equipment or facility conditions, overexertion and heat stress. Injuries can range from sprains, cuts and broken bones to concussions, torn ligaments, heart problems, paralysis or, rarely, death. I understand these risks, I am taking part voluntarily, and I accept them.

3. My health

I confirm that I am physically able to play full-court basketball and have no medical condition that makes it unsafe for me to do so, or that I have checked with a doctor first. I will stop playing and tell my coach or a league official if I am hurt or feel unwell.

4. Release and waiver of claims

To the fullest extent the law allows, I release and agree not to sue Smithtown Gospel Tabernacle, the Smithtown Gospel Tabernacle Basketball League, and their pastors, staff, officers, league administrators, coaches, officials, scorekeepers and volunteers (together, the “League Parties”) for any injury, loss or damage to me or my property that arises from my participation, including injuries caused by the ordinary negligence of a League Party or of another player. This release does not cover gross negligence or intentional misconduct. I also agree to be responsible for any claim brought against the League Parties because of my own actions.

5. Emergency medical care

If I am injured and can’t speak for myself, I authorize league officials to call emergency services and share the emergency contact and medical notes I provided with responders. I am responsible for the cost of any treatment. The league does not provide medical or accident insurance; I should rely on my own health insurance.

6. Personal information

The following will be shown publicly on the league website: my name, team, jersey number, position, height, weight, shooting hand, game results and statistics. My email, phone number, emergency contact, medical notes, faith answers and any fee-assistance request are private. Only the league office sees them; they are never published or shared with coaches, and are used only to run the league and contact me.

7. Photo and video release

Every game is filmed, and the film is processed by the league’s video-stats provider to produce stats, highlights and box scores. I give Smithtown Gospel Tabernacle, the Smithtown Gospel Tabernacle Basketball League and their video-stats provider permission to record, use and publish photos, video and audio of me taken at league games and events, and my name and statistics, on the league website, in game recaps and highlights, and in church and league communications, without payment. I can ask the league office to take down a specific photo or clip, and it will make a reasonable effort to do so.

8. Age and signature

I am 18 or older and have read and understood this document. If I am under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to it on my behalf before I can play. My checkbox and the details I submit with this registration serve as my electronic signature.

Code of Conduct

The Smithtown Gospel Tabernacle Basketball League is a ministry of Smithtown Gospel Tabernacle. We play hard and we play to win, but we play in a way that honors God and each other (Colossians 3:17). Everyone who plays agrees to the following.

1. Respect everyone

  • Treat teammates, opponents, officials, scorekeepers, volunteers and spectators with respect, in person and online.
  • No trash talk aimed at a person, taunting, threats, or language that is profane, crude, racist, sexist or demeaning.
  • Shake hands, or tap fists, after every game, win or lose.

2. Respect the officials

  • Officials’ calls are final. Only a team’s coach may ask an official about a call, calmly and during a stoppage.
  • Arguing, gesturing at or confronting an official is unsportsmanlike and will draw a technical foul.

3. Play safe, clean basketball

  • No fighting, punching, shoving after the whistle, or deliberately dangerous play.
  • Games are 5-on-5. Regular-season games can end in a tie (there’s no overtime); playoff games go to 5-minute overtime periods until there’s a winner.

4. Technical fouls, flagrant fouls and ejections

OffenseResult
Technical foul (unsportsmanlike conduct, arguing a call, taunting)Opponent shoots free throws. Recorded on your league record.
Second technical foul in the same gameEjection from that game.
Flagrant foul (excessive or dangerous contact)Ejection. The league office reviews the film and may add a suspension.
EjectionLeave the gym promptly; suspended for your team’s next game.
Fighting, or threatening an official or playerImmediate ejection and suspension, up to removal from the league without a refund.
Repeated technical fouls during a seasonReviewed by the league office; may lead to a suspension.

5. Honesty and fair play

  • Only registered, rostered players may play. Teams keep a 9-player active roster and up to 3 players on the Reserve List.
  • Report your weekly availability honestly so coaches can plan.
  • All roster moves go through the Team Portal: trades are player-for-player, both coaches must confirm, and the League Admin approves every trade. Free agents are claimed with sealed FAAB bids. No side deals, no money changing hands, and no tampering with other teams’ players.
  • Don’t share private information you see in the Team Portal, such as other players’ availability.

6. Respect the church and the gym

  • We are guests on church property. No alcohol, tobacco, vaping, drugs or weapons on the premises, and don’t show up impaired.
  • Wear your league jersey and non-marking basketball shoes. Clean up after your team and leave the gym the way you found it.
  • Arrive on time and ready to play.

7. Online and on film

  • Games are filmed and stats are public. Conduct caught on film can be reviewed by the league office, even if it was missed during the game.
  • Don’t post insults or attacks about players, teams or officials on social media or in league group chats.

8. Enforcement and appeals

  • The League Admin enforces this code and may issue warnings, suspensions or removal from the league depending on how serious the conduct was.
  • A player can appeal a suspension in writing to the league office within 48 hours. Penalties stand while an appeal is reviewed.
  • Disagreements should be handled privately and directly first (Matthew 18:15), then with your coach, then with the league office.
