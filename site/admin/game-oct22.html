Brown scores 30, Team Jeremy beats Team Turbo 94-60 in season opener

Frisco, Texas · Thursday, Oct. 22, 2026

Bryson Brown had 30 points, eight rebounds and seven assists to lead Team Jeremy past Team Turbo 94-60 on Thursday night in the season opener for both teams.

Brown made 11 of 16 shots from the field, 4 of 7 from 3-point range and 4 of 5 free throws.

Jeremy Jones added 20 points and seven assists, and Santonio Martin had 13 points and 13 rebounds.

Team Jeremy shot 53.7% (36 of 67) from the field and made 16 3-pointers.

Team Turbo fell to 0-1. Lamar Sharper led Team Turbo with 16 points and seven rebounds.

Devon Edwards added 11 points.

Team Jeremy never trailed. A 14-0 Team Jeremy run, capped by Jeff Pritchard's 3-pointer, made it 27-9. Team Jeremy was up 53-25 midway through the game.

Team Jeremy led by as many as 38. Jeremy Jones and Jermiah Johnson scored Team Jeremy's final 6 points.
